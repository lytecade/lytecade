## About this game

CyberBreaker is a crisp, responsive brick-breaker built with pure HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript. Styled with the Arne16 cyberpunk palette, it challenges you to clear escalating waves of neon blocks without letting the energy ball slip past your paddle.

You begin each run with 5 lives and earn points for every block shattered and wave cleared, with your high score tracked at the end of the run.
