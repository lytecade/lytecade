## About this game

Bounce Mallow is an endless procedural auto-runner platformer built from scratch with HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript. You play as an animated marshmallow running forward automatically—timing your jumps is everything.

The world generates ahead of the camera in chunks with guaranteed reachable floating rock platforms, forged steel spikes, patrolling armored spike bugs, and rare Espresso Cup and Chamomile Flower speed powerups. Distance traveled in meters is tracked live, with your personal best saved locally in your browser.
