## About this game

Ikonoku brings a visual twist to classic Sudoku by replacing digits with 9 distinct colors from the Arne16 palette. Fill every row, column, and 3×3 region so each contains all 9 color blocks exactly once—pure visual logic with no math required.

Choose between Easy, Medium, and Hard puzzle boards and play seamlessly with either mouse/touch controls or full keyboard navigation.
