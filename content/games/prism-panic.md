## About this game

Prism Panic is a fast-paced memory match flash card game rendered on HTML5 Canvas using the Arne16 color palette. At the start of each round, all 16 cards are revealed briefly for 3 seconds before flipping face down.

Flip pairs of cards to match all 8 unique shape and color combinations before the 5-minute timer expires. Every remaining second boosts your final score while missed pairs apply a small penalty.
