## About this game

Syndicate Sky 3884 is an arcade space shooter inspired by *1942*, built with an 800×800 HTML5 Canvas, CRT scanline aesthetic, and the Arne16 palette. Blast through escalating waves of hostile corporate fleets while weaving through relentless bullet patterns.

Chain enemy kills for score multipliers, collect weapon upgrades, shields, and extra lives, and prepare for powerful syndicate boss encounters every fifth wave.
