## About this game

CyberStack is an arcade tower-stacking game styled with neon Arne16 visuals and particle slice effects. Each new slab slides back and forth above your tower—drop it at the right instant to align with the block underneath.

Any overhanging edge is sliced off, shrinking your landing surface for the next drop. Score one point per stacked block and beat your persistent browser high score before a block misses completely.
